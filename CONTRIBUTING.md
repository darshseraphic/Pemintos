## Report a bug

Send a report to **Darshseraphic** at [Darsh.seraphic@gmail.com](mailto:Darsh.seraphic@gmail.com). Include:

1. A short, descriptive title.
2. The Pemintos version (currently `1.1.0`) and your Chrome version.
3. Your operating system.
4. The steps needed to reproduce the issue.
5. What you expected to happen and what actually happened.
6. Any relevant error message, with private information removed.

Please do not attach the contents of personal notes, passwords, authentication tokens, browser cookies, or other private data. A small example using fictional text is usually enough.

## Suggest an improvement

Feature suggestions are welcome. Explain the problem or use case first, describe the behavior you would expect, and mention any relevant accessibility, privacy, or usability considerations. Keep the proposal focused so it is easy to understand and evaluate.

## Help test the extension

You may test an official, unmodified copy for personal evaluation. When reporting a result, mention the Chrome version and the exact steps you tried. Useful areas to check include:

- Drafts are restored after closing and reopening the popup.
- Saving a note adds it to the Saved notes list and starts a TXT download.
- Automatic and custom filenames behave as expected.
- Saved notes open in the full-page editor and remain available after saving edits.
- The theme selector cycles through System, Light, and Dark.
- Keyboard shortcuts behave as described in the README.

Do not include private note contents in testing reports.
