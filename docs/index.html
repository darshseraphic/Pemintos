THE IDEA

Less managing.
More remembering.

Pemintos keeps the act of taking a note simple. Your draft stays in Chrome while you work. When a thought is ready to keep, save it to your notes library and download a text copy for wherever you need it next.

06 / QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

Will my draft still be there when I close the popup?

Yes. Pemintos saves the current draft in Chrome's local extension storage as you type. Reopen the popup to continue where you left off.

Where are my saved notes stored?

Saved notes live in Pemintos' local extension storage, not in a regular folder on your computer. Downloaded TXT files are separate and follow Chrome's download settings.

Can I edit a note after saving it?

Yes. Select the note in Saved notes to open the full-page editor. Save changes to update the in-extension copy, or download the edited note as a TXT file.

Can I choose the filename?

Yes. Pemintos suggests a filename based on the first word of your note. Use the custom filename control to provide a different name.

Does Pemintos encrypt my notes?

No application-level encryption is currently added. Notes are stored through Chrome's local extension storage. Do not use Pemintos as a password manager or for highly sensitive information.
